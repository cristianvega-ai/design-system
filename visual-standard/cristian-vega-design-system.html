Contrast and rules

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines AA on 9–16 px text
  • Do · Azure text on surface. 5.0:1 on surface, 4.5:1 on the well. Links, link-like buttons, the note-band kicker.
  • Do · Teal text #0A7A64 on its tint. 4.6:1 on teal-bg, 5.3:1 on surface, 4.7:1 on the well. The teal dot keeps #0C8B72. A dot is not text.
  • Do not · Sky text on surface. 2.8:1 on surface, 2.5:1 on the well. On surface, sky is a dot, a dash, or a hash mark. Never a word.
  • Do · Mist text on ink. #FFC69E reaches 11.7:1. Use it where azure text would sit on ink.
  • Do not · Azure text on ink. 3.6:1. Buttons keep the azure fill with white text on both grounds.
  • Do · Muted #586173 on surface. 6.2:1 on surface, 5.6:1 on the well. Safe for 12 px metadata.
  • Do · Meta #5F697B on surface. 5.5:1 on surface, 5.0:1 on the well. The floor for 9–11 px labels.
  • Do · A control boundary at 3:1. Input, select, textarea, check, radio, and the switch track read --g-line-control. Text buttons need no perimeter.
  • Do · Controls on surface and on the well. The boundary reads 3.3:1 on surface and 3.0:1 on the well. The ground test shows it.
  • Do not · A semantic hue for an action. Teal never means "go". A destructive button is a red ghost, not a red fill.
  • Do not · A lone sky or amber dot on surface. 2.8:1 and 3.2:1 on surface, 2.5:1 and 2.9:1 on the well. Put a word next to the dot, or put the dot on ink.

Copy

Voice · one word per state
  • Do · Sentence case. Buttons, titles, and body text. Mono labels are uppercase.
  • Do not · Title Case a button. "Revoke key", not "Revoke Key".
  • Do · A verb and its object on a button. "Revoke key". "Keep key". "Generate note".
  • Do not · Write "OK", "Yes", or "Submit". The button says what happens.
  • Do · One word per state, everywhere. idle · queued · streaming · needs you · done · failed · cancelled · offline. The chip, the dot label, the act state, the sidebar dot, and the Meta sample use the same word.
  • Do not · Rename a state. "running", "waiting", and "live" are streaming and needs you under other names. A gate runs. An agent streams.
  • Do · Say what happened, then what to do. An error has two lines. An empty state has one line and one action.
  • Do not · Use an exclamation mark. Or a joke. Or "oops".

Rule · every user-facing string follows Simplified Technical English. Short sentences. Active voice. One instruction per sentence. The same word for the same thing.

Motion & focus

Five movements. Nothing else moves.

Entrances rise. Cards lift. Live elements pulse. Unknown progress sweeps. Unknown state spins. Every loop and every lift stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

Do and do not

  • Do · Name every color. Use the token. A raw hex in a view is a finding.
  • Do not · Add a fourth face or a fourteenth size. Three faces and thirteen sizes cover display, reading, and values.
  • Do · Put one signal button in a view. The second action is a ghost.
  • Do not · Fill a selection without the rail. The rail marks a selection in a sidebar, a list, a table, and a palette. A raised tint can sit under it. In a list, surface with the level 1 shadow can sit under it. In a palette, surface-2 can sit under it. The segmented control is the one exception. Its active segment is a surface fill with no rail.
  • Do · Set mono on every machine value. Dates, counts, identifiers, paths, models, shortcuts.
  • Do · Use one word per state. is-active is the current place. is-selected is one of many. is-open is a disclosure. Use them for visual state a product sets from its own model, never in place of a native state.
  • Do not · Write a pixel. Space, radius, type, shadow, and duration have names. A raw number in a view is a finding, the same as a raw hex.
  • Do not · Write sky words on surface. Sky is a dot, a dash, or a hash on surface.
  • Do · Number a real sequence. Gates G1–G5, steps 01–08. Nothing else.
  • Do not · Decorate. No illustrations, no emoji markers, no glass. Gradients fill rules, dashes, dots, meters, the highlight, the seal panel, and the drop-zone is-over tint (grad-soft). Never a resting surface.
